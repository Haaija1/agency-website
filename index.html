Why Weave

Your tools should work together.

When a business grows, the work between its systems grows too: copying information, chasing updates, and checking the same details twice. Weave connects those steps so your team can spend more time on work that needs their judgement.

Workflow Example

From scattered updates to one clear view.

An illustrative workflow: bringing incoming job details, a shared tracker, and team updates together. This shows an approach we can discuss for your business, rather than a measured client result.

01

Capture the details once

A new enquiry or job arrives. The workflow collects the required details and uses a consistent reference to keep related information together.

02

Check before updating

Required fields and duplicate entries are checked. Missing or unclear information is sent to a person for review before the record moves forward.

03

Give the team a shared view

The agreed tracker is updated and the right person is notified. The team can see the next action without chasing the same update in several places.

Project 01

Multi-branch payroll consolidation

  • Multi-branch retail
  • Payroll & commission
  • Client name withheld

A multi-branch retail business pulled its payroll together by hand every month from WhatsApp messages, a shared spreadsheet, and bank statements. We built an automation that consolidates all three, and while we tested it against the business’s own historical numbers, it caught a real commission error.

01

The problem

Attendance arrived as free-text WhatsApp replies from branch managers, hours lived in a shared spreadsheet, and commission depended on reading a bank statement by hand, including subtracting refunds. Small errors were easy to miss until a payslip was already wrong.

02

What we built

A structured intake that files each input as it arrives, a separately tested calculation step for each source, and one firm rule: anything the system isn’t certain about is flagged for a person to review, never silently guessed. The payroll output is always checked by a person.

03

The result

Checked against the business’s own historical figures, the automation found a commission error in one branch’s numbers that had gone unnoticed, before it could reach a payslip.

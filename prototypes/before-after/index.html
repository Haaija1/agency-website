The same job, before and after a redesign

One supplier invoice, from the inbox to a payment. In the before drawing the threads cross and double back: the same figures are typed in twice, people chase each other, and the invoice waits. In the after drawing those threads merge into one flow, with a person still approving the payment.

Illustrative example, not a client result

Scrolling through this section also plays the merge.

A person approves every payment. Nothing in the after drawing approves or pays on its own: the redesign removes the re-typing, the chasing and the waiting, and leaves the decision where it belongs. The two numbers above are a count of the boxes drawn here and nothing else.
